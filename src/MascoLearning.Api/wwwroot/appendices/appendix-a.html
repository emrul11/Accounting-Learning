Appendix A · Desk reference

Bangladesh RMG Glossary

Plain-language definitions of the terms you will hear daily — each tied to the module where it lives in depth.

A — C

TermPlain meaning at MascoModule
AITAdvance Income Tax — others withhold from our receipts (bank LC settlements, imports); becomes prepaid tax credit.14
Accrual basisRecording income/costs when EARNED/incurred, not when cash moves. The whole site runs on this.6, 8
Accumulated depreciationRunning total of an asset's cost already expensed; cost minus this = book value.10
AR / Accounts ReceivableMoney buyers owe us for shipped garments — tracked buyer-wise and invoice-wise in a subledger.3, 9
AP / Accounts PayableMoney we owe suppliers for received goods/services.3, 9
Aging reportPromises sorted by days outstanding (0-30/31-60/61-90/90+); collections radar and compliance clock.9
B/L (Bill of Lading)Carrier's document proving shipment. THE revenue date for exports.6, 7
Back-to-back LCImport LC issued against the export LC's proceeds — finances yarn while hedging currency naturally.7
Bond licensePermission to import inputs duty-free on the promise they become exports.14
C&F agentClearing & Forwarding agent — handles customs/port formalities for shipments.7
COA / Chart of AccountsThe master list of every account: code + name + type. The company's filing cabinet.2
COGSCost of Goods Sold — the accumulated true cost of exactly the units that SHIPPED.5, 6
Control accountA GL account whose balance must equal its subledger's total (e.g., 1310 AR—Export).3
Credit noteDocument reducing a buyer's receivable (claims/settlements) — never silent edits.13

D — G

TermPlain meaning at MascoModule
Debit / CreditLeft side / right side of every entry. NOT good/bad, NOT in/out. DEA-LER decides effects.1
Double-entryEvery event recorded from both sides (value went somewhere FROM somewhere); debits = credits always.1
EPBExport Promotion Bureau — receives export statistics/declarations from exporters.7, 14
FX gain/lossDifference between booked rate and settlement rate; realized = permanent, unrealized reverses.7
Finished goods (FG)Completed garments in warehouse — still an ASSET until shipped.6
FIFO / Moving averageMethods deciding which cost layer leaves when material is consumed; Masco uses moving weighted average.6
GSPGeneralized System of Preferences — duty benefits for EU exports needing origin proof.7, 14
GRN (Goods Received Note)Warehouse document proving goods arrived, quantity-checked, QC'd. No GRN → no entry → no payment.0

I — P

TermPlain meaning at MascoModule
JIT vs bulk importInventory strategy choices affecting storage, bond norms, and cash flow timing.6, 9
LC (Letter of Credit)Bank's payment promise: pays against compliant DOCUMENTS after shipment. Sight = immediate; usance = 60/90/120 days.7
Maker-checkerSensitive actions stay pending until a DIFFERENT authorized person approves.12
NBRNational Board of Revenue — Bangladesh's tax authority. Rates change every June budget.14
Posting engineThe machine converting APPROVED documents into journal entries via versioned rules R1–R9.4
ProvisionExpense recognized NOW for probable future obligations (bonus, WPPF, doubtful debts).8, 9

R — U

TermPlain meaning at MascoModule
ReconciliationProving two records agree (subledger=GL, books=bank statement). Daily for ledgers, weekly for bank.3, 12
Reversal entryThe ONLY way to correct a posted entry — original stays visible forever.4, 12
Segregation of dutiesNo one person holds two of: authorize / execute / record for the same flow.12
SubledgerDetail layer beneath a GL control account: per buyer/supplier/invoice/lot rows.3
TDSTax Deducted at Source — WE withhold from payments (salaries, vendors) and deposit to NBR.8, 14
Three-way matchPO + approved GRN + supplier invoice agreeing before ANY payment releases.9, 12
UD (Utilization Declaration)Customs declaration proving bonded imports became exports within permitted wastage.14

V — W

TermPlain meaning at MascoModule
VAT Input / OutputVAT we pay on purchases (recoverable asset) vs collect on sales (state's money we hold). Exports: zero-rated.14
WIP (Work In Progress)Half-made goods sitting in process buckets between yarn and FG.5
WPPFWorkers' Profit Participation Fund — statutory profit share provisioned annually.8, 14
Write-down / write-offDocumented removal of value (damaged stock, uncollectable debts) through approvals — never deletion.6, 13
Usage tip: when any term feels shaky in a meeting, jump to its module via the third column — definitions here are reminders, understanding lives in the lessons.
