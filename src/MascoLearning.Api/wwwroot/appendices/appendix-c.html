Role-Based Quick Guides

What you PROVIDE to accounting, what you USE from it, and the top mistakes your role must never make.

📋 Commercial Officer — Nasrin

You provide: complete LC files, B/Ls matching invoices word-for-word, timely export invoices, buyer claim correspondence with full evidence.

You use: AR aging (whose payment to chase), realization status vs the 120-day window, FX position on open receivables, gross margin per style from costing sheets.

Your top 3 mistakes to never make

1. Invoice/B/L mismatches (creates LC discrepancies = frozen cash).
2. Slow document collection pushing realization past windows.
3. Negotiating below true cost because costing sheets weren't consulted.

Your modules: 0 → 6 → 7 → 9 → 13. Deep reads later: 5, 11.

🏭 Warehouse / Production — Jamal & supervisors

You provide: same-day accurate GRNs, issue slips tied to orders/lots, honest output reports, damage/scrap documentation, bonded-vs-local stock segregation.

You use: system stock levels for planning, GRN approval status (unblocks supplier payments), count schedules.

Your top 3 mistakes to never make

1. Late GRNs (3 days of lying reports + stock-out surprises).
2. Wrong item/lot classification at receipt (wrong debits forever).
3. Approving your own receipts or adjusting your own stock counts (Module 12 toxic pairs).

Your modules: 0 → 5 → 6 → 13. Deep reads later: 1, 4.

💰 Finance / Accounts — Shirin's team

You provide: rule-table authorship (with sign-offs), reconciliations daily/weekly/monthly, statements that agree three ways, exception and aging vigilance, tax calendars met.

You use: every subledger, the posting engine's rule versions, audit trails, budget-cycle rate tables.

Your top 3 mistakes to never make

1. Manual GL journals bypassing subledgers ("fixing" totals — THE unforgivable shortcut).
2. Paying unmatched invoices under pressure scripts.
3. Letting month-close release before the three statements reconcile to each other.

Your modules: ALL, in order. No skipping.

👥 HR / Payroll — Mizan

You provide: department-coded employee masters updated on transfer day, biometric-backed attendance, pre-authorized OT tied to output, bonus/WPPF registers worker-wise.

You use: TDS certificates issued per employee, payroll cost splits by department, provision schedules.

Your top 3 mistakes to never make

1. Stale department codes silently rerouting salaries between product cost and admin.
2. OT hours without matching production output (audit magnet).
3. Late joiner/leaver sync creating ghost or missing payrolls.

Your modules: 0 → 8. Deep reads later: 12, 14.

💻 Software Developers / Implementers — Rafiq's team

You provide: the posting engine (config-versioned rules), atomic GL+subledger writes, SoD enforcement, immutable audit logs, dated tax-rate config, statements computed from ledgers.

You use: this ENTIRE site as the requirements' WHY; the future developer spec for HOW.

Your top 3 mistakes to never make

1. Entries without source-document references (Golden Rule violation in code form).
2. Non-atomic writes letting GL and subledger diverge.
3. Hardcoded rates/accounts/rules — anything a June budget or new factory would break silently.

Your modules: ALL — twice. Second pass while designing schemas.
