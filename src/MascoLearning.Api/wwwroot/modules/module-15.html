Capstone: One Month at Masco

"Can you close one full month at the factory?"

The Mystery

Shirin has gone on a four-day Eid holiday. She left a single sentence on the office whiteboard, circled three times in red:

"Close March 2026. Every document on my desk. Don't ask anyone else to clean up after you."

On the desk: an in-tray of sixteen real-looking factory documents — LCs, GRNs, invoices, payroll sheets, supplier statements, bank vouchers, one suspicious-looking duplicate, and a production completion certificate. Your job: turn the paper trail into journal entries, reconcile the supplier, post closing adjustments, and produce a tiny income statement and balance sheet. By the end of this module, you will have closed a month at Masco.

There are no dumb questions
Why are two of these documents contradictory?
Because factories are messy. INV-0991 is a duplicate invoice from a supplier trying (or accidentally) to double-bill. The XYZ Mills statement claims a delivery we cannot find on the receiving floor. Closing a month means spotting both before any money leaves — exactly what controls (Module 12) and exceptions (Module 13) are designed for.
Do I need to journalize every document?
No. You journalize EVENTS, and a document is the proof an event happened (Module 0). The duplicate invoice is not an event — it is a fraud attempt / data-entry error. The supplier statement is not an event — it is someone else's version of our events. You journalize what your documents prove, then reconcile against their statement later.
What is "weighted average" yarn cost?
Module 5/6: when several receipts of the same item sit in inventory together at different buy prices, we revalue the whole batch to its weighted average before issuing. Here both lots landed at ৳620/kg, so the average is simply ৳620.

Your move — predict each adjustment

What is missing from the trial balance that must be accrued or reclassified to make March's books tell the truth?

Task 5 — Explain the Results in Plain Language

Predict — write one paragraph first

Shirin comes back from Eid and asks: "We made about ৳60,000 profit this month, but our bank balance fell by roughly ৳58 lakh. Explain that in two sentences a non-finance colleague can understand."

Model answer (one possible phrasing):

"We earned ৳59,500 of profit this month, but cash fell by around ৳58 lakh because most of the month's cash went out for yarn inventory, supplier payments, and salaries — while the ৳63,75,000 of revenue from H&M is still on the road as a receivable, waiting to be paid by their bank. Profit is what we earned on paper under accrual accounting; cash is what is actually in our account. They will converge when H&M pays through the LC."

Why this matters: managers and directors often confuse the two. The reconciliation between profit and cash movement is called the working-capital story (Module 11), and it lives in the AR / AP / Inventory lines on the balance sheet. This month, receivable went up ৳63,75,000 — that's the missing cash.

What This Means for Your Job

Rafiq — Developer

Sequence validation is your real product

Three-way match (PO + GRN + Invoice) at payment time is the code path that would have blocked the duplicate. Missing-GRN flagging at statement-receipt time is the code path that would have caught the XYZ Mills claim. Build those two gates before you build anything else.

Nasrin — Commercial

You are the first line of duplicate detection

When a second invoice for the same shipment lands in your inbox, the system should refuse to book it — but your eyes spot it first. Same for supplier statements that include items not on any GRN you have ever issued.

Jamal — Warehouse

The GRN is the whole supply chain's anchor

A GRN created the day yarn lands is worth 10x one created a week later. Every late GRN is a window where inventory exists physically but not in the books — and the supplier statement becomes a trap waiting for a careless payment.

Shirin — Finance

You sign the close

Month-end is reconciliation work, not data-entry work. Your job is to chase every gap between your books and supplier statements until it is explained in writing — and to refuse to post entries that are not backed by documents.

Mizan — HR/Payroll

TDS is a precision instrument

The payroll entry (Dr Salaries Expense / Cr TDS Payable + Cr Cash) is a three-line instrument: get any one amount wrong and either workers are underpaid or the NBR liability is wrong. Treat it like a check — eyes open, every line.
